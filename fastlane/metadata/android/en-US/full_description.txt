Velum is an independent, open-source companion for the Plaud Note Pro.

• Talk to your recorder over BLE — no Velum cloud
• Bring your own OpenAI-compatible API (or local models)
• Transcription, templates, and notes stay on your device
• Phone microphone recording with a proper foreground service

Not affiliated with Plaud Inc. Source: https://github.com/bloodf/velum
