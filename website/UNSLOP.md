# Unslop review

## Direction

- **Product:** Velum is an open-source AI notetaker for desktop and mobile, built for people who capture conversations across apps, AI notetakers, and external recorders.
- **Character:** Calm, exact, tactile.
- **World:** Paper, studio glass, and captured sound.
- **Moments:** The official dimensional Velum mark and the real desktop and mobile product interface.
- **Signature:** A live waveform carried from the hero into the product story.

## Audit

Light slop. Keep the existing palette, type families, section order, glass surfaces, and real product visuals.

- Uppercase tracked metadata becomes quieter and more readable.
- Italic emphasis is unnecessary where weight, color, and the serif family already create hierarchy.
- The floating logo loop and device parallax add motion without explaining the product.
- Small metadata needs a modest readability increase.
- Floating hero notes repeat information already present in the navigation and compatibility story.

## Polish passes

- Spacing and layout: preserve the established composition and section order.
- Color and icons: preserve the neutral brand ramp, real logo, and existing icon set.
- Copy and type: remove italic emphasis and improve metadata readability.
- Details: remove redundant floating hero notes.
- Visuals: retain the official 3D mark and all real product screenshots.
- Motion: remove the decorative float loop and device parallax; retain meaningful sound and scroll-story motion.

## Verification

- Production build passed.
- Browser and accessibility tests: 9/9 passed.
- Desktop and mobile previews inspected after the polish pass.
- Palette, typography families, section order, official brand assets, and real product screenshots preserved.
